GrailTrackrr - setup
====================

One plugin plus two small mods. Each mod has its own save folder, so the characters
you track stay apart from everything else you play.

  GrailTrackrr       your Holy Grail
  GrailTrackrrRace   races and challenges

Saves go to:
  Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected\mods\GrailTrackrr\
  Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected\mods\GrailTrackrrRace\

The plugin only tracks while one of these two mods is running. With no mod or any
other mod, it stays off and sends nothing.


SETUP
-----
1. Install D2RLoader (https://d2rloader.net) and check the game starts with it.

2. Drag the "d2rloader" and "mods" folders from this zip into your game folder
   (the folder with D2R.exe). If Windows asks, choose to merge the folders.

3. Sign up at https://grailtrackrr.com, open Profile and copy your API key.

4. In D2RLoader, open General > Startup and set "Default mod" to GrailTrackrr.
   Leave "Allow mod extensions" on. Start the game once, then close it.

5. Open <game folder>\d2rloader\config\grailtrackrr.toml in Notepad and paste
   your key between the quotes:  api_key = "your key"

6. On the site, start a grail. In game, make a new level 1 character (the shared
   stash of a new mod starts empty). Your grail goes live on its own.

The bottom-left corner shows "GrailTrackrr connected (Grail)" when it works.
The console command "grailtrackrr" shows the status too.


ALREADY ON A GRAIL?
-------------------
Copy your grail characters (.d2s files) and your shared stash file
(SharedStashSoftCoreV2.d2i or SharedStashHardCoreV2.d2i) from
  Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected\
into
  Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected\mods\GrailTrackrr\
and keep going. Copy only characters your grail already has: any other
character is flagged. Your api_key from before stays as it is.


RACES AND CHALLENGES
--------------------
Join the event on the site, set "Default mod" to GrailTrackrrRace, and make a new
level 1 character when the event starts. Nothing from races counts toward your
grail.


GOOD TO KNOW
------------
- Playing without these mods (no mod, or another mod) is not tracked.
- Other mods (D2RMM and similar) are not active while GrailTrackrr is the mod.
- Updating: drag the new folders in again. Your settings and key are kept.
- Help: https://github.com/pyrosplat/grailtrackrr-plugin/issues
